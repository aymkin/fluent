# DUO A2 exam format

Load from `/fluent-listening`, `/fluent-reading`, `/fluent-writing` and
`/fluent-speaking` when the round runs in exam mode (the default). The four
skills build their rounds on this file; it is the single home of the format. A
number with no source below is not established.

Sources: official page `inburgeren.nl/examen-doen/inhoud-taalexamens-a2-b1-b2.jsp`
(time, form); the three official writing practice exams
`inburgeren.nl/images/oefenexamen-schrijven-1.pdf` (also `-2`, `-3`); the public
practice exams of DUO, listed on `inburgeren.nl/examen-doen/oefenen.jsp`
(Spreken, Luisteren, Lezen, 2024 editions), read on 2026-10-19. Rubrics: DUO's
`beoordelingsmodel` documents, summarised in
`docs/research/2026-10-06-a2-exam-prep.md` §2.

The exam items are © Ministerie van SZW. The skills copy the **form** of an item
— situation line, instruction, question stem, option count — and write their own
content.

## The four exams

| Exam | Time | Items | Marked by |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Luisteren | 45 min, computer | 25 questions on 10 fragments, 2–3 questions each | computer |
| Lezen | 65 min, computer | 25 questions on about 10 texts, 2–3 questions each | computer |
| Schrijven | 40 min, **pen on paper** | 4 tasks, one per page | assessors |
| Spreken | 35 min, computer with microphone | 16 spoken answers in 4 blocks of 4 | assessors |

The pass mark is not published; Lezen and Luisteren give no partial credit
beyond right or wrong.

## Luisteren

- **Fragment**: a conversation on video, a clip from the internet, a phone call
  (audio only), the news, or a voicemail. Settings seen: a street, a
  neighbourhood centre, work, a course, the children's school, a hospital.
- **Frame** shown with the fragment, two lines of situation, then the
  instruction: `Steven komt Jari tegen op straat. Ze praten met elkaar.` and
  `Lees eerst de vraag. Kijk daarna naar de video.` (`Luister daarna naar de
  tekst.` for audio). The question is also read aloud.
- **Options**: three (A–C) in 21 of 25 questions, four in 4. One question asks
  for a picture: `Kies het juiste plaatje.`
- **Questions** ask a detail (how long, when, where, who, why, how much, which)
  or what someone must do. None asks what the conversation is about.
- The practice player has play, pause and ±10 s buttons. Whether the live exam
  lets a candidate hear a fragment again is not published.

## Lezen

- **Text**: a short, structured everyday text. Genres seen: tips in a course
  guide, a letter to residents, a shop folder (transport service), a letter from
  work, an email from a colleague, a letter from the neighbourhood association,
  an email from a party committee, a course folder, a newspaper item, house
  rules (calling in sick).
- **Frame**: one situation line (`Sabrina krijgt een brief van haar werk.`),
  then `Lees eerst de vraag. Lees daarna de tekst.` Text on the left, question on
  the right.
- **Question stem** opens with a mini-situation about a named person and asks for
  one fact: what to bring, when, whom to ask, how much, which course fits, what
  to do. Two questions in 25 ask why the text was written.
- **Options**: three (A–C) in 14 of 25 questions, four (A–D) in 11.

## Schrijven

Four tasks. Types seen across the three practice exams (the order varies):

1. **Email** to a teacher, colleague, boss or fellow student, from 3–4 bullet
   points. The header (to, from, subject) and the salutation and closing are
   printed. Bullets are things to write (`Schrijf waarom u wilt ruilen. Bedenk
   het zelf.`). `Schrijf in hele zinnen.`
2. **Neighbourhood paper** (`wijkkrant`): at least three sentences about a
   celebration, clothes or the weekend, three guiding questions.
3. **Form** (`formulier`): course sign-up, police report, report to the
   council. Personal fields, one or two free-text fields, a choice. `Sommige
   gegevens moet u zelf bedenken.` Part of the form takes its content from
   pictures.
4. **Second email or a note** to a colleague from pictures: `Schrijf drie dingen
   op.`

A candidate invents what the task leaves open. The instructions address the candidate as `u`.

## Spreken

Four blocks of four questions each.

| Block | Prompt | The answer |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 Video | a person asks a question aloud, no text | answer the question |
| 2 One picture | situation line, `Vertel … Vertel ook …`, `Gebruik het plaatje.` | describe, then the second part (opinion, reason, how often, what to do next) |
| 3 Two pictures | situation, `Vertel … Vertel ook waarom.`, `Kies een van de plaatjes.` | choose one, give a reason or a time |
| 4 Three pictures | `Vertel iets over alle plaatjes.` / `Gebruik alle plaatjes.` | one thing about each picture |

Every prompt has two parts, and the answer owes both. One prompt asks for a
count (`Noem drie dingen.`).

## Rubrics

| Aspect | Writing task and neighbourhood paper | Form | Speaking |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Adequaatheid (task done, understandable) | 0–3 | 0–3 | 0–3 |
| Grammatica | 0–2 | | 0–2 |
| Spelling | 0–2 | 0–2 | |
| Woordgebruik | 0–2 | 0–2 | 0–2 |
| Coherentie (en, maar, want, omdat) | 0–1 | | 0–1 |
| Vloeiendheid | | | 0–2 |
| Uitspraak (accent allowed) | | | 0–2 |
| Maximum | 10 | 7 | 12 |

- An adequaatheid of 0 gives 0 for every other aspect.
- Grammar is judged as a whole by error count (`veel`, `redelijk wat`,
  `nauwelijks of geen`).
- A writing answer that uses almost only words from the task earns 0 for
  woordgebruik.
- Two points for every aspect and every bullet covered is a sensible target;
  it is not an official pass mark.
